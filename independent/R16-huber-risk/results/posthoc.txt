3. When does the Huber bias vanish? It is zero whenever kappa exceeds the largest deviation of the target from its
   Huber root, i.e. roughly kappa > M(1-p). At p=0.3 the jump is 3.7 (g=0.1) and kappa=3 is nearly inactive; at p=0.05 the
   jump is 22 and even kappa=3 removes 80-95% of the risky arm's expected premium. For DQN with kappa=1 and reward
   clipping to [-1,1], Huber is inactive on reward noise and active only on *value* jumps: terminations from high-value
   states and rare large bootstrapped values.

4. DQN check (results/dqn/verdict.txt): K4 passed 13/16. Failure-Q (true value 1) ends at 26.8 under Huber vs 22.2 under the
   squared loss; only 2% of TD errors exceed kappa=1, but those are exactly the terminal ones. Return is equal at the end
   (129 vs 131) and the squared loss has higher AUC (146 vs 125). Huber's protective reputation on CartPole is not visible
   here; its cost on the terminal values is.

   F1 failed for a reason worth stating: a one-hot MLP is not a table. Its hidden and output layers are shared, so a state with no data still receives a value through the biases and the random untrained row of W1. The gap is bridged by every encoding; the smooth one bridges it at exactly the full-coverage speed (7850 = the no-gap T_p at K=200), the one-hot one 'bridges' it fastest by skipping it.

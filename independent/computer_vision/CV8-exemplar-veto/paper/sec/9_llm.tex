\section*{LLM Usage Statement}
\label{sec:llm}

A large language model was used extensively in this work: Claude Fable 5.2, run as an AI coding assistant inside the Cursor IDE with access to the authors' workstation.
Its use covered the following parts.
\emph{Ideation.} After a literature search, the assistant proposed the research question, the experimental design, and the preregistered criteria and their thresholds.
\emph{Code.} It wrote the experiment, analysis and plotting code.
\emph{Experiments.} It launched the runs on the authors' GPU and collected their outputs; all numbers in the paper are computed by scripts from the stored outputs of those runs.
\emph{Related work.} It searched for and summarized prior work; the bibliographic data of every reference were checked against arXiv or publisher records.
\emph{Text, tables and figures.} It drafted all sections of the paper, including this statement, and generated the tables and figures.
The authors directed the work, reviewed the code, the results and the text, and take full responsibility for the content of the paper.

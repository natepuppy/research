# CV10: Localized but not recognized — SAM 3's presence head vetoes the small objects its queries already found

**Question.** SAM 3 scores every proposal as the product of two numbers: the proposal's own match score and one image-level presence score that says whether the named concept is in the image at all. The paper motivates the split by saying recognition needs global context and localization is local, so one global token does the recognizing. The claim here is that the split puts SAM 3's small-object failure in one place. For a small object, the proposal queries still find the object and score it high, and the presence token, which reads a 1008-pixel view of the whole image, says the concept is absent, so the product falls under the threshold and the object is dropped. If that is where the failure lives, two things follow. First, misses on small objects are mostly recognition misses with the object already localized, not localization misses, and the share of such vetoes rises as objects get smaller while the matched proposal's own score does not fall with size the way the presence score does. Second, the fix needs no retraining and no mask merging: compute presence on a few crops of the image, take the maximum, and apply it to the proposals from the single full-image pass. Lowering the global threshold is the usual remedy and it cannot do this, because the same threshold that admits a vetoed small object admits every absent-concept false positive whose product sits in the same range. Tiling the whole pipeline (run the decoder on crops, map masks back, merge) is the practitioners' remedy; it also recovers small objects, at the price of duplicate masks, seam artifacts and a merge step. Local presence takes the recognition from the crops and the localization from the full pass.

Prior work: SAM 3 (presence token; decoupled recognition and localization; the final score is the product; 1008-pixel fixed-resolution input with aspect ratio not preserved). "Confidence scores in open-vocabulary detection are a biased mixture of scale and semantics" (2026) finds that CLIP-based detectors (GroundingDINO, OWL-ViT, YOLO-World) systematically under-score small objects and that threshold selection cannot recover them; it attributes the bias to image-level pretraining of the region-text similarity. SAM 3's two-factor score makes it possible to ask which factor carries the scale bias, which that paper's detectors cannot answer. Tiled inference for SAM 3 is folklore (GitHub issue 464, several engineering write-ups) with no measurement of what tiling recovers and what it costs on absent concepts, and no separation of its recognition and localization effects. CV8 in this repository used the text-only presence as a gate; CV8 phase 2 found text-only presence negative for 8 % of present LVIS categories, which is the population this paper decomposes.

**Status.** Measured on 500 LVIS val images on the 3080 (SAM 3 fp16, 1,536 present pairs, 3,401 ground-truth instances of which 1,405 small, 2,917 verified-absent episodes, 46 minutes while sharing the GPU). **KILLED under the pre-registered test: K1 0/8, K2 0/8, K3 0/8.** The mechanism is the reverse of the one claimed. The presence head is size-invariant: presence < 0 for 8.4 % of pairs whose largest instance is small, 10.0 % medium, 7.1 % large; Spearman of presence with log area is 0.025 over pairs and −0.031 over localized instances; the median presence logit is 4.4 / 4.2 / 4.3 by bin. The proposal queries carry the scale bias: among instances that some proposal localizes at IoU > 0.5 (88.9 % of small, 98.9 % of medium, 98.7 % of large), the median matching proposal's own score is 0.75 small, 0.89 medium, 0.95 large, Spearman with log area 0.55. Of 524 small misses at the 0.5 threshold, 20.8 % are presence vetoes, 49.4 % are localized proposals with their own score ≤ 0.5, 29.8 % are not localized. Local presence (max over five crops) adds 1.8 points of small recall (0.627 → 0.645) and raises verified-absent false positives from 0.8 % to 1.3 %; full tiling with merge reaches 0.704 at 1.6 % with 1.8× the kept masks. At local presence's false-positive rate, lowering the global threshold to 0.315 gives small recall 0.732, ten points more than the proposed fix and three more than tiling.

What the stored scores say (post-hoc, not pre-registered; `results/real/threshold_curve_posthoc.csv`). The presence head does nearly all of SAM 3's rejection: on verified-absent categories, presence ≥ 0 in 1.1 % of episodes, while the best proposal's own score exceeds 0.5 in 68.8 % and exceeds 0.3 in 98.6 %. Because the two factors are independent and the scale bias is only in the second, lowering the product threshold trades small-object recall against absent-concept false positives at a favorable rate: τ = 0.5 → 0.2 raises small recall 0.627 → 0.784 and overall recall 0.741 → 0.862 while absent false-positive episodes go 0.8 % → 2.3 %; τ = 0.1 gives 0.837 small recall at 4.3 %. This is the opposite of what "Confidence scores in open-vocabulary detection are a biased mixture of scale and semantics" reports for CLIP-based detectors, where lowering the threshold floods the output with false positives and oracle per-scale thresholds gain nothing on small objects. The difference is the presence head: it holds the absent-concept false positives at a rate that does not depend on τ. Not run: SA-Co, per-category thresholds, any model other than SAM 3, a check of whether SAM 3's reported LVIS AP-fixed would move under a lower operating point (AP is threshold-free; cgF1 at 0.5 is not).

## Pre-registered kill test (written before any measurement)

Design. LVIS v1 val annotations on the COCO val2017 images on disk: the first 500 images by id that have at least one verified-absent category (`neg_category_ids`), at least one polygon instance of area ≥ 32² px, and at least one exhaustively annotated present category. SAM 3 fp16 from `facebook/sam3`, score threshold 0.5, mask threshold 0, phrase = the category's first synonym with underscores replaced by spaces. Per image, the **present** categories are the exhaustively annotated present categories ordered by total area, up to 6; the **absent** categories are up to 6 verified-absent categories drawn with a fixed per-image seed. Size bins follow COCO on original pixels: small < 32², medium 32²–96², large > 96². A proposal **matches** a ground-truth instance when mask IoU > 0.5.

Passes. **Full**: one vision pass on the whole image, one decoder pass per category; store the presence logit, every proposal's logit and mask. **Crops**: five crops, each 0.6 w × 0.6 h, at the four corners and the center, so every pixel is in at least one crop and the crops overlap; one vision pass per crop and one decoder pass per category per crop; store the crop presence logit and, for the tiling baseline, every proposal's logit and its mask mapped back into full-image coordinates.

Instance outcomes, from the full pass alone, for every ground-truth instance of a present category. **Hit**: a kept proposal (σ(logit)·σ(presence) > 0.5) matches it. Otherwise a miss, split three ways: **veto** if a proposal matches it with σ(logit) > 0.5 (it would have been kept had presence been +∞); **weak** if a proposal matches it but every matching proposal has σ(logit) ≤ 0.5; **unlocalized** if no proposal matches it. Per (image, category) pair: the presence logit, the size bin of the largest instance, and the best matching proposal's σ(logit) for that largest instance.

Fixes, all evaluated on the same proposals and the same absent categories. **Local presence**: presence* = max(full presence, max over crops of crop presence); score = σ(logit)·σ(presence*) on the full pass's proposals; nothing else changes. **Threshold**: full pass, threshold lowered to the largest τ ≤ 0.5 at which the verified-absent false-positive episode rate (any kept mask for an absent category) equals or exceeds local presence's; computed from stored scores. **Tiling**: full-pass proposals plus every crop proposal mapped back, each scored by its own pass's product, kept at 0.5, then greedy mask-IoU non-maximum suppression at 0.5 across the pool. Recall is the fraction of ground-truth instances matched by a kept mask, by size bin. False-positive rate is the fraction of absent (image, category) episodes with any kept mask. 8 bootstrap resamples of images; a criterion passes if it holds in ≥ 6 of 8.

- **K1 (the veto is a small-object effect).** Among present (image, category) pairs, the fraction with presence < 0 is ≥ 0.20 when the largest instance is small and at least 3× the fraction when the largest instance is large. And the dissociation: the drop from large to small in the fraction of pairs whose largest instance has a matching proposal with σ(logit) > 0.5 is less than the rise from large to small in the fraction with presence < 0.
- **K2 (small-object misses are vetoes).** Among missed small instances, the veto share (veto / (veto + weak + unlocalized)) is ≥ 0.40.
- **K3 (local presence recovers them without opening the gate).** Local presence raises small-instance recall by ≥ 10 points absolute over the full pass, raises the verified-absent false-positive episode rate by ≤ 5 points absolute, and at the matched false-positive rate beats the threshold baseline's small-instance recall by ≥ 5 points.

SUPPORTED if K1, K2 and K3. INCONCLUSIVE if exactly two. KILLED otherwise. Reported, not scored: medium and large recall under each method (local presence should not lower them); tiling's recall, false-positive rate and kept-mask count; the fraction of vetoed small instances whose crop presence turns positive; the recall and false-positive rate of local presence when only the center crop or only the four corner crops are used.

What would make this a null: small-object misses being mostly unlocalized (the queries fail with the presence head, so the decoupling is not where the scale bias lives); crop presence firing for absent concepts as often as it fires for vetoed small objects (local presence pays for its recall in false positives and the threshold baseline matches it); or presence being as negative for large-object pairs as for small ones (the veto is about something other than size, which CV11 tests).

2. Sign of over_dhat: mean over all Q-hats = -0.918; fraction negative (underestimation) = 0.88
   Zero-initialised tabular Q-learning with restarts under-estimates on the way up; the learners that are further behind
   (small alpha, large eps) under-estimate more AND have worse policies, so 'more overestimation' correlates with BETTER
   performance here (rho = -0.65). The sign is the opposite of the deep-RL story and is a time-of-learning effect, not a bias effect.

5. Reading. In this corpus the errors learning produces are diffuse (every state is visited under eps-greedy with restarts),
   so every norm is a monotone proxy for 'how far along is this learner', and all of them predict performance at 0.72-0.78.
   The gap-crossing statistic is better (0.93) because it ignores error that does not flip an action, but the margin is
   0.15 over the best norm, not the regime change the hypothesis claimed, and weighting by the policy's own visitation
   adds only 0.02. The hypothesis was motivated by errors concentrated off the policy's path (function approximation,
   narrow behaviour data); the pilot did not produce such errors and so could not test that regime.

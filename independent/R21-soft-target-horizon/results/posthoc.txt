P3 what the law says about the default tau=0.005 (n=1): per-segment delay 200 updates plus 1/alpha_eff. With an ideal critic (alpha=1) a decision 100 steps from its consequence sees it after ~20k updates; with n=5, ~4k. The alpha_eff of a replay-trained neural critic is not 1 and is the quantity the 3080 experiment must measure (regress the learned V on truncated MC returns to read h(t) directly).
